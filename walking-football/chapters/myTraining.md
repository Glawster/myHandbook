# Personal Walking Football Training Plan

Version: 1.0
Started: July 2026

---

# Objectives

This training plan is designed specifically for me to improve my walking football performance while working within my current health and mobility.

## Primary Goals

- Improve walking football fitness
- Increase stamina without running
- Improve balance and stability
- Improve agility and turning speed
- Improve first touch and ball control
- Build strength for injury prevention
- Lose excess weight gradually (if applicable)
- Continue enjoying walking football into later life

# Training Principles

- Low impact only
- No running
- Quality movement over intensity
- Consistency beats occasional hard sessions
- Increase workload gradually
- Stop if knee pain increases
