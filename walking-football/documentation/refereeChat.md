Morning/afternoon everyone.

Just a couple of things before we start.

**Walking** – please keep it to walking. If I think you're running, I'll call it. It's hard to judge whether you had one foot on the ground or not, so if it looks like running I'll blow it.

**Contact** – keep tackles controlled. No tackling from behind, no barging and no going through a player to get the ball. If you're behind someone, don't try to nick the ball through their legs — get alongside them.

**Height** – keep the ball below the permitted height. If it goes over, or is dangerous, I'll give the appropriate restart.

**Free kicks and distance** – when I give a free kick, give the required distance straight away and let the game restart.

Most importantly, we're here to enjoy the game. I'll explain decisions where I can, but once I've made the decision, let's get on with it.

Have a good game.
