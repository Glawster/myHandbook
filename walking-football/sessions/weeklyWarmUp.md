# HWFC Weekly Walking Football Warm-Up

**Routine:** Walk → Mobilise → Pass → Possess → Play  
**Duration:** Approximately 15–17 minutes  
**Purpose:** Prepare players safely for walking football while reinforcing first touch, scanning, passing and movement.

## Equipment and setup

- Four cones to mark a square approximately **15 × 15 metres**.
- Two footballs for the passing drill; one ball for possession.
- Space for players to stand comfortably apart during mobility exercises.
- A stable support for anyone who needs help balancing during single-leg movements.

## Weekly schedule

| Stage | Activity | Time |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Gentle walking | 2 minutes |
| 2 | Dynamic mobility and stretching | 5 minutes |
| 3 | Pass, Move, Scan | 5 minutes |
| 4 | Possession warm-up | 3–5 minutes |
| **Total** | | **15–17 minutes** |

## Stage 1 – Gentle walking (2 minutes)

Walk around the playing area at a comfortable pace, increasing gradually to a purposeful walking speed. Add gentle changes of direction, avoiding sharp turns. Encourage relaxed shoulders and natural arm movement.

## Stage 2 – Dynamic mobility and stretching (5 minutes)

Arrange players in a line or semicircle with room to move. Demonstrate each exercise. **Do not bounce or force stretches.** Movements should be slow, controlled and pain-free.

| Exercise | Time | Instructions |
|---|---:|---|
| **1. Ankle rotations** | 45 sec | Lift one foot slightly; rotate the ankle five times clockwise and five times anticlockwise. Repeat on the other side. Hold a stable support if necessary. |
| **2. Heel raises** | 45 sec | With feet shoulder-width apart, rise gently onto the toes, then lower the heels. Repeat about 10 times. |
| **3. Alternating knee lifts** | 60 sec | March on the spot, raising alternate knees to a comfortable height. Stay upright and controlled. |
| **4. Hip and leg swings** | 60 sec | Holding a stable support, swing each leg gently forwards and backwards, then side to side, approximately five times in each direction. Reduce the range for comfort. |
| **5. Shoulder rolls and torso turns** | 45 sec | Roll the shoulders five times forwards and five backwards. Gently rotate the upper body from side to side without forcing the back. |
| **6. Side steps** | 45 sec | Take three or four controlled steps sideways, then return in the opposite direction. Keep the knees soft. |

> **Coaching note:** These are dynamic mobility exercises rather than prolonged static stretches. Save longer held stretches for the cool-down.

## Stage 3 – Pass, Move, Scan (5 minutes)

### Setup

1. Place four cones in a square approximately 15 × 15 metres.
2. Position one or more players at each corner.
3. Start with two balls at opposite corners, provided the group can keep the sequence organised.

### Basic sequence

1. A player **looks up and scans** before receiving.
2. Receive the pass and take the **first touch towards the next target**.
3. Pass to the next corner in a clockwise direction.
4. **Walk after the pass** to join the line at the next corner.
5. Continue so that players are receiving, passing and moving regularly.

### Progression

- **Minutes 0–2:** Comfortable passing and first touch; establish the pattern.
- **Minutes 2–3:** Encourage two-touch play: control, then pass.
- **Minutes 3–4:** Reverse direction to practise a different body shape and foot.
- **Minutes 4–5:** Receiver moves a step off the cone to create a passing angle before receiving.

**Coaching cues:** *Scan early. Open your body. First touch into space. Pass accurately. Move after passing.*

## Stage 4 – Possession warm-up (3–5 minutes)

In or around the same square:

- Place approximately **4–5 players around the outside** and **2–3 players inside**, with **one defender** trying to intercept.
- The team in possession uses the outside players and moving inside players to keep the ball.
- Players should continually offer a passing angle rather than stand still.
- Rotate the defender frequently, allowing a short recovery between turns.
- Adapt the numbers and size of the square to the attendance and mobility of the group.

Keep this controlled: it is still a warm-up, not a full-intensity competitive exercise.

**Coaching cues:** *Create space. Communicate. Support the ball carrier. Pass and move. No running.*

## Weekly coaching focus

Use the **same routine each week** while varying just one theme:

| Week | Main emphasis |
|---|---|
| 1 | First touch and control |
| 2 | Scanning before receiving |
| 3 | Passing accuracy and weight |
| 4 | Moving to create angles |
| 5 | Opening the body to receive |
| 6 | Communication and simple one-twos |

Repeat or change the themes as needed. Once familiar, a nominated player can lead the mobility section while the coach sets out the cones.

## Optional cool-down (3 minutes)

After training or a match:

1. **Gentle walking (1 minute):** Gradually reduce the pace.
2. **Calf stretch:** Hold a comfortable position for roughly 15–20 seconds per side.
3. **Hamstring stretch:** Hold gently for roughly 15–20 seconds per side.
4. **Quadriceps stretch:** Hold with support for roughly 15–20 seconds per side, or substitute a comfortable alternative where balance or knee movement is limited.

Avoid bouncing, and do not stretch into pain.

## Safety and accessibility

- Keep the entire session appropriate to walking football: **walking, not jogging or running**.
- Encourage players to work within a comfortable range of motion; no one needs to match another player's height of lift or swing.
- Offer balance support or seated alternatives where appropriate.
- Avoid sudden twists, forced knee bends or painful movements.
- Players recovering from injury or with significant mobility or joint concerns may need individual adjustments.
- Stop activity if a player develops chest pain, dizziness, unusual breathlessness or acute pain, and seek appropriate help.
- Check the surface, footwear and spacing before beginning.
