# Goalkeeper Training Session – Walking Football

## Aim

Improve goalkeeper:

- Set position
- Reactions
- Movement across goal
- Handling
- Decision-making
- Distribution

**Session length:** 45–60 minutes

---

## 1. Warm-up and Handling

**Duration:** 8 minutes

Start with the goalkeeper 3–4 metres from a server.

### Drill

- 10 catches at chest height
- 10 low catches
- 10 balls alternated left and right
- Progress to slightly quicker service

### Coaching Points

- Hands ready in front of the body
- Knees slightly flexed
- Weight on the balls of the feet
- Move the feet before reaching
- Get the body behind the ball wherever possible

---

## 2. Set Position and Lateral Movement

**Duration:** 10 minutes

Mark three positions across the goal:

- Left
- Centre
- Right

The goalkeeper starts centrally.

### Drill

1. Call a position.
2. The goalkeeper moves quickly into line with that cone.
3. As soon as they arrive, play a controlled shot.

### Progression

Remove the verbal call and move the ball yourself so the goalkeeper has to read your position.

### Key Habit

**Move → Set → Save**

The goalkeeper should avoid still travelling sideways when the shot is struck.

---

## 3. Reaction Saves

**Duration:** 10 minutes

Stand approximately 4–5 metres away with several footballs.

### Drill

Play controlled shots randomly:

- Low left
- Low right
- Central
- Shoulder height
- Occasionally slightly behind the goalkeeper’s initial hand position

Give another ball fairly quickly after each save so the goalkeeper has to recover and reset.

### Progression

Use a tennis ball from closer range before returning to footballs.

### Coaching Points

Focus on the goalkeeper’s first movement.

They should react with both hands and feet rather than simply throwing one hand towards the ball.

---

## 4. Close-Range Positioning

**Duration:** 10 minutes

Have an attacker receive the ball around 5–7 metres from goal.

The attacker can take one or two touches before shooting.

### Coaching Points

Teach the goalkeeper to:

- Adjust position as the attacker moves
- Avoid standing permanently on the goal line
- Reduce the available target
- Avoid getting so close that the attacker can easily pass around them

### Progression

Have the attacker receive with their back to goal, turn and shoot.

This is particularly relevant to walking football.

---

## 5. Rebounds and Second Saves

**Duration:** 8 minutes

The server shoots deliberately so that some saves are difficult to hold.

### Drill

The goalkeeper must:

1. Make the first save
2. Recover their balance
3. Locate the ball immediately
4. Either collect it or prepare for a second shot

### Coaching Point

A save is not finished until the danger has gone.

---

## 6. Distribution

**Duration:** 10 minutes

Place three players or target gates at different distances and angles.

After making a simple save, the goalkeeper distributes immediately to a called target.

### Work On

- Short accurate roll
- Controlled throw
- Simple pass with the feet where competition rules allow
- Selecting the safer player rather than automatically playing forward

### Progression

Stop calling the target and allow the goalkeeper to decide where the best outlet is.

### Key Principle

**The simple pass is usually the good pass.**

The goalkeeper does not need to create an attack with every distribution.

---

## 7. Final Game

**Duration:** 8–10 minutes

Set up:

**3 attackers v 2 defenders + goalkeeper**

Start every repetition with the attackers in possession.

Once the goalkeeper gains the ball, their objective is to distribute successfully to a target player outside the area.

### What This Develops

- Positioning
- Communication
- Saving
- Recovery
- Distribution
- Decision-making under pressure

---

## Goalkeeper Habits

Throughout the session, keep reinforcing:

**Ready → Move → Set → Save → Reset**

When the team has possession:

**Look → Decide → Distribute**

---

## Key Walking Football Principle

Spend plenty of time on positioning before the shot.

A goalkeeper who consistently gets into the correct position will often appear to have excellent reactions because many saves become straightforward rather than spectacular.
