# Session 3 – Turning Under Pressure

**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Players:** 8–16

**Theme:** Turning Under Pressure

**Key Phrase:** **Scan. Protect. Turn.**

---

## Objectives

By the end of the session players should be able to:

- Scan before receiving.
- Protect the ball under pressure.
- Decide whether to turn or play safely backwards.
- Turn using controlled walking steps.
- Avoid twisting on a planted foot.

---

## Equipment

- Footballs
- Cones
- Bibs
- Two mini goals
- Flat markers

---

# Warm-up (10 Minutes)

## Ball Familiarisation

Each player has a ball inside a marked area.

Walking only.

Practise:
Coach calls what touch to be using from these:
- Inside touches
- Outside touches
- Sole rolls
- Push-pulls
- Stop the ball with the sole
- Gentle changes of direction
- Looking up between touches

After a few minutes, the coach calls a players name:

**Scan!** All players turn and point to that player

### Coaching Points

- Keep the ball close.
- Walk with small controlled steps.
- Scan regularly.
- Turn the whole body, not just the knees.

### Common Mistakes

- Looking only at the ball.
- Heavy touches.
- Twisting sharply.
- Trying to move too quickly.

---

# Passing Rondo (10 Minutes)

## Objective

Develop accurate passing, good first touch and constant scanning.

## Setup

Groups of 5–7.

One defender inside an 8 × 8 metre square.

Outside players keep possession.

### Instructions

- Walking only.
- Two touches where possible.
- Scan before receiving.
- Rotate the defender every minute or when possession changes.

### Progressions

- Unlimited touches.
- Two touches.
- One touch.
- Add a second defender.

### Coaching Points

- Scan before receiving.
- Open your body.
- Pass with the correct weight.
- Move after passing.
- Communicate.

### Common Mistakes

- Standing still.
- Watching only the ball.
- Passing too hard.
- Closing passing angles.

---

# Technical Practice 1 (10 Minutes)

## Receive and Turn Through a Gate

### Objective

Develop scanning before receiving and turning into space.

### Setup

Pairs.

Passer approximately five metres away.

Two gates behind the receiver.

### Instructions

1. Receiver scans.
2. Passer waits until the scan is completed.
3. Pass is played.
4. Coach calls a gate.
5. Receiver turns through the gate.
6. Return to the start.

### Progressions

- Coach calls the gate before the pass.
- Coach calls during the pass.
- Receiver chooses the gate.

### Coaching Points

- Scan before receiving.
- Receive side-on.
- Cushion the first touch.
- Turn with several small walking steps.

### Common Mistakes

- Standing square.
- Turning before scanning.
- Heavy first touch.
- Pivoting on one foot.

---

# Technical Practice 2 (10 Minutes)

## Protect or Turn

### Objective

Help players decide whether to turn, shield or play backwards.

### Setup

Groups of three.

- Passer
- Receiver
- Passive defender

Defender starts **1–1.5 metres** behind the receiver.

### Instructions

1. Receiver scans.
2. Passer plays the ball.
3. Defender applies light pressure.
4. Receiver decides whether to:
   - Turn.
   - Shield.
   - Return the pass.

Rotate regularly.

### Progressions

- Defender starts closer.
- Defender becomes more active.
- Reduce available space.

### Coaching Points

- Feel where the defender is.
- Keep the ball on the far side.
- Don't force the turn.
- A safe pass is often the correct decision.

### Common Mistakes

- Turning automatically.
- Turning into pressure.
- Holding the ball too long.
- Pivoting sharply.

---

# Technical Practice 3 (10 Minutes)

## Four-Gate Turning Square

### Objective

Develop scanning, orientation and turning into space.

### Setup

10 × 10 metre square.

One gate in the centre of each side.

One server.

One receiver.

The server changes sides after every repetition.

### Instructions

1. Server moves to a new side.
2. Receiver scans to locate the server and all four gates.
3. Server passes.
4. Coach calls a gate (or receiver chooses later).
5. Receiver turns through the gate.
6. Returns the ball.
7. Server moves to another side.

Swap roles regularly.

### Progressions

- Gate called before the pass.
- Gate called during the pass.
- Receiver chooses.
- Passive defender added.

### Coaching Points

- Scan before every pass.
- Open the body.
- First touch towards the chosen gate.
- Small walking steps.
- Pass accurately after turning.

### Common Mistakes

- Forgetting to scan.
- Standing square.
- Heavy first touch.
- Deciding too late.
- Pivoting on one foot.

---

# Match Practice (10 Minutes)

Normal walking football.

### Conditions

- Walking only.
- No tackling from behind.
- Goals scored after a successful turn count double.
- Praise good decisions not to turn.

Allow play to flow.

Coach mainly through encouragement and brief interventions.

---

# Cool Down (5 Minutes)

- Gentle walking.
- Calf stretch.
- Hamstring stretch.
- Hip-flexor stretch.
- Chest stretch.
- Controlled breathing.

---

# Coach's Q&A (5 Minutes)

- Why do we scan before receiving?
- When is it better **not** to turn?
- How do we protect the ball?
- Why are several small steps safer than pivoting?
- Which drill helped you most today?
- What will you try in next week's match?

---

# Key Takeaways

- **Scan** before receiving.
- **Protect** the ball when under pressure.
- **Turn** only into space.
- **Support** your teammates after passing.
- **Good decisions are more important than fancy turns.**
