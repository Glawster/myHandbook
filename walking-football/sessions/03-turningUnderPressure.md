# Session 3 – Turning Under Pressure

**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Players:** 8–16

**Theme:** Receiving, protecting and turning safely.

**Key Phrase:** **Scan. Protect. Turn.**

## Objectives

By the end of this session, players should be able to:

- Check over their shoulder before receiving.
- Protect the ball when under pressure.
- Decide whether to turn or play a safe return pass.
- Turn using small, controlled walking steps.
- Avoid twisting sharply on a planted foot.

## Equipment

- Footballs
- Cones
- Bibs
- Two mini goals
- Flat markers

## Warm-up (10 Minutes)

### Ball Familiarisation

Each player has a ball inside a marked area.

Walking only.

Practise:
Coach calls what touch to be using from these:
- Inside touches
- Outside touches
- Sole rolls
- Push-pulls
- Stopping the ball with the sole
- Changing direction using several small steps
- Looking up between touches

After a few minutes, the coach calls a players name:

**Scan!** All players turn and point to that player

### Coaching Points

- Keep the ball close.
- Use small steps when changing direction.
- Look up regularly.
- Turn the whole body rather than twisting through the knee.

### Common Mistakes

- Looking down continuously.
- Heavy touches.
- Pivoting on one planted foot.
- Trying to turn too quickly.

---

## Technical Practice (10 Minutes)

### Receive and Turn Through a Gate

#### Setup

Players work in pairs.

Two gates are positioned behind the receiver.

```text
       Left Gate        Right Gate

          ||               ||

              Receiver

                 ●

              Passer
```

The passer stands approximately five metres away.

#### Instructions

1. The receiver checks over both shoulders.
2. The passer plays the ball having seen the over the shoulder check.
3. The receiver controls the ball, passer calls the gate.
4. Turn through called gate[text](../../.mypy_cache).
5. Walk forward with the ball.
6. Reset and repeat.

Practise turning in both directions.

#### Progression

The passer points towards one gate immediately before passing.

The receiver must:

- Scan.
- Recognise the signal.
- Turn through the opposite gate.

### Coaching Points

- Scan before receiving.
- Receive side-on where possible.
- Cushion the first touch.
- Turn using several small walking steps.
- Keep balanced.

### Common Mistakes

- Standing square.
- Turning before scanning.
- Heavy first touch.
- Twisting on a planted foot.

---

## Practice 2 – Protect or Turn (10 Minutes)

### Setup

Groups of three.

- Passer
- Receiver
- Passive defender, 1.5m behind receiver

The defender applies light pressure only.

### Instructions

1. Receiver scans.
2. Passer plays the ball.
3. Defender closes down at slow to walking pace.
4. Receiver decides whether to:

- Turn.
- Protect the ball.
- Return the pass.

Rotate roles regularly.

### Coaching Points

- Feel where the defender is.
- Keep the ball on the far side.
- Use your body for protection.
- Turning is only one option.
- A safe return pass is often the best decision.

### Common Mistakes

- Turning automatically.
- Turning towards pressure.
- Holding the ball too long.
- Pivoting sharply.

---

## Practice 3 – Four-Gate Turning Square (10 Minutes)

### Setup

Create a square approximately 10 × 10 metres.

Place one gate in the centre of each side.

One server starts outside the square with a ball.

One receiver stands in the middle.

```text
               Gate

        +----------------+
        |                |
  Gate  |       R        |  Gate
        |                |
        +----------------+

               Gate
```

The server moves to a different side of the square after every pass.

### Instructions

1. The server chooses a position outside one side of the square.
2. Before the pass, the receiver scans to locate both the server and the four gates.
3. The server passes the ball into the receiver.
4. The coach calls a gate, or later allows the receiver to choose the best option.
5. The receiver controls the ball and turns through the selected gate.
6. After passing through the gate, the receiver turns and passes the ball back to the server.
7. The server moves to a different side of the square before the next repetition.

After several repetitions, swap the receiver and server.

### Progressions

#### Level 1

The coach calls the gate before the pass.

#### Level 2

The coach calls the gate as the ball travels.

#### Level 3

The receiver decides which gate offers the most space.

#### Level 4

Add a passive defender who applies light pressure after the first touch.

### Coaching Points

- Scan before every pass, even if you think you know where the server is.
- Open your body to receive side-on.
- Receive with your first touch towards the chosen gate.
- Use several small walking steps rather than a sharp pivot.
- Keep the ball close throughout the turn.
- Look up before passing back.

### Common Mistakes

- Forgetting to scan before receiving.
- Standing square to the server.
- Taking a heavy first touch.
- Turning before deciding where to go.
- Pivoting on one planted foot.

## Directional Possession Game (10 Minutes)

### Setup

4 v 4 or 5 v 5.

Two end zones.

### Scoring

One point is scored when a player:

1. Receives with their back or side to goal.
2. Turns successfully.
3. Walks or passes into the end zone.

### Conditions

- Walking only.
- No tackling from behind.
- Bonus point for scanning before receiving.
- Bonus point for protecting the ball before passing.

### Coaching Challenge

Ask players:

> Can I turn?

If not:

> Can I protect?

If not:

> Can I play backwards safely?

### Coaching Points

- Receive side-on.
- Check both shoulders.
- Turn only into space.
- Support after passing.

---

## Match Practice (10 Minutes)

Normal walking football.

### Conditions

- Walking only.
- No tackling from behind.
- Goals scored after a successful turn count double.
- Praise good decisions not to turn under pressure.

Avoid stopping play too often.

Allow players to recognise opportunities themselves.

---

## Cool Down (5 Minutes)

- Gentle walking
- Calf stretch
- Hamstring stretch
- Hip-flexor stretch
- Chest stretch
- Slow breathing

Avoid twisting through the knees.

---

## Review Questions

Ask the players:

- What should you do before receiving?
- When is it better not to turn?
- How do you protect the ball?
- Why are small steps safer than a pivot?
- Which turn felt most comfortable today?

---

## Session Summary

**Focus:** Turning under pressure.

**Key Learning:** Scan before receiving and only turn when space is available.

**Safety:** Turn using several small walking steps rather than twisting on a planted foot.
